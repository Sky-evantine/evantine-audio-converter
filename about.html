01 / THE FOUNDER

Sky Evantine.
Artist. Builder. Founder.

Sky Evantine is the creative force behind Evantine Apparel — a New York-based artist and multidisciplinary creator whose work moves between clothing, music, modeling, production, and visual storytelling.

Known in music as $oul Fire, Sky writes, sings, and produces original music. City Tech's 2025 student spotlight describes his path from a small town in western New York to New York City and his studies in Entertainment Technology, where performance, audio, video, lighting, and stage production meet. Read the City Tech feature ↗

Evantine grows from that same instinct: make things, keep moving, and give people room to make the work their own.

02 / CREATIVE PRACTICE

MODEL / MUSIC / PRODUCTION / DESIGN

A creative life,
in motion.

Modeling, music, production, and fashion are not separate worlds here. They are different ways of telling a story — through sound, silhouette, movement, image, and atmosphere.

Sky's modeling work is part of that larger practice, bringing the same attention to image and movement into Evantine's visual language.

03 / THE MISSION

No one deserves
the shadows.

Everyone has a spotlight.

Evantine believes creativity should make room for people to be seen, heard, and fully themselves. That means clothing can be more than a uniform: it can be a place for identity, expression, confidence, and movement.

Sky also supports mental-health awareness and the importance of making space for people who may feel unseen or alone. The mission is simple: create work that brings people toward the light, not deeper into the shadows.
